## Featured Research & Preprints

* **Thermodynamic Waste Heat Optimization in GreenVolt Systems**
  * *Identifier:* [Read Business Case (PDF)](./path-to-your-file.pdf)
  * *Abstract:* Educational infrastructure generates substantial unsegregated solid waste while incurring high climate-control energy costs. GreenVolt is an integrated waste-to-energy recovery system combining computer-vision automated classification with on-site aerobic composting and thermoelectric heat harvesting. Primary sorting utilizes optical, infrared, and mass sensors to maximize recycling purity. Exothermic heat from organic decomposition is captured via phase-change storage and Seebeck-effect thermoelectric generators (TEGs). Thermodynamic modeling indicates an annual carbon offset of 2,460 kg CO₂e against an operational footprint of 1,396 kg CO₂e (a 176% net-positive offset ratio). Deployment yields a 30–40% reduction in landfill waste, a 25% improvement in sorting efficiency, and a 10–15% offset in local power demands.
* **Mathematical Modeling and Adsorption Kinetics of Agricultural Byproduct Biofilters**
  * *Identifier:* [Read Article on Substack](https://athenamanjooran.substack.com/p/mathematical-modeling-and-adsorption?r=61vree&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web&showWelcomeOnShare=true)
  * *Abstract:* Agricultural byproduct biofilters offer a low-cost, decentralized alternative to energy-intensive water purification systems. By utilizing semi-charred sunflower seed husks, banana peels, and coconut husks, this research evaluates adsorption kinetics and surface complexation mechanisms for heavy metal (Cu²⁺) removal and turbidity reduction. Using Langmuir adsorption isotherm models, empirical results demonstrate removal efficiencies up to 95% while reducing operating costs to $0.02–$0.10 per m³ of water treated.
